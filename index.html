About Us

We are a global team of passionate students from MIT’s Emerging Talent program, brought together by curiosity and a challenge. What began as a class project quickly grew into something more: a shared mission to make cybersecurity knowledge accessible to everyone.

Our vision is simple: empower people to recognize and stop phishing attacks before they succeed. We believe learning doesn’t have to be technical or complicated; it can be practical, engaging, and even fun.

Beyond individuals, we are committed to helping small and medium enterprises with 25-50 employees protect themselves with affordable, high-quality training programs. Together, we’re building not just research, but a knowledge base that anyone, anywhere, can use.

Research Findings

This study analyzed linguistic and structural patterns of phishing emails versus legitimate ones. Using a dataset of 29,710 Enron emails, we tested 22 linguistic, readability, and psychological features with statistical methods and a Random Forest model.

Results showed negative sentiment and certain structural cues are strong indicators of phishing, with combined features achieving over 82% accuracy. External validation with Nazario data confirmed robustness at 88.5% accuracy.
